We're shipping **0.4.13-alpha**, the release that lifts the toolchain pin. Until
now, KetoyVM forced your app onto its exact Kotlin / AGP / Compose combination.
That's gone. **0.4.0 decouples your project from KetoyVM's pinned toolchain on
two independent axes.**

### Coordinates

<CodeWindow file="build.gradle.kts">
{`dependencies {
    implementation(platform("dev.ketoy.vm:ketoy-bom:0.4.13-alpha"))
    implementation("dev.ketoy.vm:ketoy-runtime")
    implementation("dev.ketoy.vm:ketoy-hilt")            // optional
    implementation("dev.ketoy.vm:ketoy-adapters-material3")
}`}
</CodeWindow>

If you're already on 0.3.x, this is a drop-in upgrade, no breaking API changes.
The headline difference is what you're now *allowed* to do: move your Kotlin,
AGP, Gradle and Compose versions without matching KetoyVM's.

### What landed

<ChipRow>
  <Chip>Embedded compiler (ADR-0004)</Chip>
  <Chip>Name-based param remap (ADR-0005)</Chip>
  <Chip>Kotlin / AGP / Gradle free</Chip>
  <Chip>Compose-version free</Chip>
  <Chip>Build-time advisories</Chip>
</ChipRow>

- **Kotlin / AGP / Gradle fully decoupled.** A Gradle worker now drives a pinned
  `K2JVMCompiler` in a classloader isolated from your build. Your Kotlin compiler
  never loads KetoyVM compiler code; KetoyVM's pinned Kotlin never sees your
  toolchain. Verified on `Devices` at **Kotlin 2.2.10 / AGP 9.1.1 / Gradle
  9.3.1**, a full AGP major ahead of KetoyVM's 8.13.
- **Compose version decoupled for the supported surface.** Parameters are now
  keyed by **name**, not position, so a newer Compose that adds, reorders, or
  removes a param no longer shifts your arguments into the wrong slot. The
  Material3 1.4.0 `Text.autoSize` insertion that used to silently break custom
  fonts now renders correctly, verified on a physical Pixel.
- **Build-time advisories.** When your Compose diverges from the catalog, the
  compiler reports exactly what it dropped (unknown param, type-mismatch) plus a
  per-bundle coverage summary, a visible advisory instead of a silent wrong
  render.
